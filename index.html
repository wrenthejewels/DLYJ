What it becomes

-

Shrinking — AI can already draft, standardize, or delegate parts of this work. The human share of these tasks compresses as tooling improves.
Retained — These tasks carry tacit knowledge, judgment, or accountability that currently resists clean substitution. They anchor the seat.
Growing — As execution thins, these higher-leverage activities take up more of the retained role. This is where the seat reinvents itself.

When it happens

The model tracks three waves of automation pressure. Each wave represents a threshold of AI capability — not a specific date, but the point at which that capability becomes commercially viable enough to change how this work gets organized.

Current wave Capabilities that exist in commercial AI tools today. This work is already under pressure from prompting, drafting, and standardization tools.
Next wave Capabilities that require better AI agents and workflow integration — likely 2–5 years out as coordination and multi-step reasoning mature.
Distant wave Capabilities requiring structural changes to how organizations staff and supervise work. Depends on social, legal, and institutional adaptation beyond the technology itself.
