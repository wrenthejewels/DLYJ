01
Overview

How we analyze your role

1

We start with the purpose for your role: what functions your role serves even if your tasks change.

Functions capture why the seat exists, not just what fills the calendar.

2

Then we derive the tasks that support those functions.

These tasks are customizable if your day-to-day role differs from the occupation baseline.

3

We score which of those tasks face automation pressure, and which ones survive it.

Task share, evidence strength, support links, and function weights combine into your role score here.

02
Pressure layer

Where AI pressure lands first

1

Tasks current AI can work on more directly

Direct pressure means current AI can already draft, standardize, or delegate parts of this work without waiting for the rest of the workflow to change first.

2

Tasks that lose value when nearby work gets compressed

These tasks often lose value because the workflow around them compresses first.
